# Service Portal: a page for leaders

**Get your link.** Your leader page opens from a personal link emailed to you. If you don't
have one (or it expired after 90 days), open the leader page from the ward's hub, type your
email and click **Email me a leader link**. Keep the link to yourself: whoever has it can act
as you on the leader page.

**Finding your way** (2.5). Tabs along the bottom: **Opportunities** (your posts; tap one to
open it), **Waiting** (people waiting for your approval, with a count), **New** (the big plus),
**Short soon** (ward council and bishopric) and **Volunteers**. When people are waiting, a
banner at the top of Opportunities says how many; tap it to go to Waiting. The **•••** at the
top right holds what isn't used every day: on the main tabs, **Sign out everywhere**; on a post,
**Edit**, **Copy the link**, **Copy this post**, **Close** and **Cancel this opportunity**.

**Create an opportunity.** Tap **New**. The form fills the screen, in four parts: **What**,
**When**, **Roles** and **Notes**, with **Save draft** and **Publish** always at the bottom. Pick the **category** first (Meals,
Moving, Service project, …; every new opportunity needs one, and its usual audiences are ticked
for you; **Copy this post** keeps the category when you may use it), then the organization, a
title, who it is for (Brothers, Sisters, Youth, Children), a public note, and any **private
details** (an address, a gate code). On the Service Opportunities page a post for one audience
shows under that audience; a post for more than one shows once, at the top under **Everyone**,
with its audiences (e.g. *Brothers · Sisters*) under the title. Then **When**:
- **Once**: one date.
- **Pick days**: tap the days on the calendar; tap again (or the **×** in the list) to remove
  one. A small gold dot marks a day that already has another opportunity, so you can spread
  them out.
- **Every week**: tap the days of the week in the one row (say **Mon**, **Wed**, **Fri**), then
  **from** and **until**. The dates appear below at once, grouped by week, each with its
  weekday. Turn a date's switch off to **skip** it (a holiday, a family away that night). At
  most 60 dates at a time.
- **Roles** are set once for every date: the role, how many people (− and +), start and end
  times if you want them, a note, and an optional question such as *What are you bringing?*
  **Add role** adds another. A date that needs something different: tap **Change** next to it
  under When; that date gets its own copy of the roles to edit (**Same as the others** puts it
  back).
- For **Youth** or **Children**, the sign-up form asks *Who is serving? Me / My child* and takes
  a child's first name. Every email goes to the parent; you see *Ana (signed up by …)*.

Never put a family's name, address or situation in a post's public note — posts are public.

**Publish and share.** **Save draft** keeps it hidden; **Publish** puts it on the Service
Opportunities page. Open the post from **Opportunities**: its public link is at the top with a
**Copy** button (also **•••** > **Copy the link**). Paste it into a ward email (addresses in
**Bcc**) or a message.

**Approve first-timers.** Someone signing up for the first time gets an email asking them to
confirm their address. Once they click it, they appear under **Waiting for approval** for the
leaders of the organization whose opportunity they first signed up for, and for the bishopric.
You can decide in either place: on the **Waiting** tab, or right on the post (open it from
**Opportunities**), where the person's line shows **Approve** and **Decline** (only to leaders
who may decide for them). Check the ward directory, then:
- **Approve**: they are approved for the whole ward. Every sign-up they have waiting counts at
  once, and each one gets its confirmation email with the private details. From then on their
  sign-ups count straight away.
- **Decline**: their waiting sign-ups end and they get a polite email asking them to contact
  the ward. They can't sign up again unless a bishopric member undoes it.
Waiting for approval lists all of their waiting sign-ups, in every organization, because approving them makes every one of those count. Your name and the time are recorded in the Volunteers tab. Approval belongs to the
organization's leaders and the bishopric only; a category coordinator doesn't approve. If
someone is still waiting 48 hours before they would serve, you get an *Urgent: waiting for
approval* email. Leaders on the ward's leader list never need approval.

**Who gets the approval emails.** The Executive Secretary can name **Approvers** for each kind
of opportunity in the Service Sheet (or one list for all of them). When a list is set, the
*Waiting for approval* and *Urgent* emails go only to the people on it. They may approve or
decline, and they see that kind's opportunities with the names (but can't edit them). The
bishopric and the organization's leaders can still approve; they just aren't emailed. With no
list, the emails go to the organization's leaders and the bishopric, as before.

**Pre-approving members.** The bishopric and the approvers can paste members' email addresses
into the **Pre-approve** list (with the bishop's permission). When one of them signs up, they
count at once: no confirm email, no waiting, and the Volunteers list shows *Pre-approve list*
as who decided. Nobody declined or revoked is ever approved this way. The addresses themselves
aren't kept, only a scrambled fingerprint of each, so the list can't be read back; to take
someone off, clear the list and paste it again without them. Someone who types a pre-approved
member's address can sign up in their name without a confirm click, but the member gets the
confirmation email and can cancel it.

**What each person's word means.** Next to each volunteer, a small ringed mark and a word:
- **Approved**: counts at once, gets the private details.
- **Leader**: on the ward's leader list; never needs approval.
- **Needs approval**: confirmed their email, waiting for a leader to approve them.
- **Email not confirmed**: hasn't clicked *Confirm my address* yet; nothing to do until they do.
- **Declined**: can't sign up until a bishopric member undoes it.
- **Revoked**: was approved, now can't sign up again until a bishopric member undoes it.

**Watch sign-ups.** Opportunities shows each post with its next date and how full it is, e.g.
**7 of 10 · 3 waiting**: 7 count, 3 are still waiting (for their email click or for approval)
and don't hold a place yet. Tap a post to see each date and role with one line per person:
their name, their mark, and what they're bringing. A post with several dates has a **day strip**
at the top (each day with its weekday and a dot, green when that day is filled): tap a day to
see only it, or **All** to see every date. You get an email when someone cancels (*You're now short* after the cancel cutoff), and
a *Fully filled* email once every role has its people.

**Over the number.** A waiting person approved after a role filled still counts: the role can
show **12 of 10**, and the person is told the need is already met and offered **Withdraw my
offer** (withdrawing sends you nothing, since the role is still full). Remove anyone you don't
need with **Remove**; they get an email.

**Private details.** They go only to approved volunteers (and leaders) who are signed up: in
their confirmation email, their reminder the day before and their **My sign-ups** page, and
only until the date is over. Never on a public page.

**What volunteers' emails offer.** The one button is **See all your sign-ups**, in the
*You're signed up* email and in the day-before reminder; cancelling, or withdrawing an offer
that came in after a role filled, is a small text link under it, *Can't make it? Cancel my
sign-up*, so the email doesn't invite people to cancel. The newest email's My sign-ups link is
the one that works.

**Needs help soon** (ward council and bishopric). Every open role still short in the next 14
days, soonest first, with a link to share. The same list is the Service Sheet's **Needs help
soon** tab.

**Coordinators.** The Categories tab can name coordinators for a kind of opportunity (a meal
coordinator, a building specialist). A coordinator sees and edits every opportunity in their
categories, whatever its organization, can make new ones there, and gets the category's
*Fully filled*, *Still short* and cancellation emails (a *Still short* email comes once per
date, at the category's Warn hours before it, when a role is still short). They don't approve
volunteers. A category with coordinators is theirs: only they, the bishopric (and the ward
council, when the category allows it) make and edit its opportunities; you can always edit
the ones you made. A category without coordinators belongs to its owning organizations'
leaders (or to any leader, when it has no owner).

**Volunteers.** Every leader has the **Volunteers** tab: everyone who has signed up, with their
mark (the red **Declined** and **Revoked** too), how many sign-ups they have coming up, their
first sign-up, and the children they signed up, listed under them. Search by name, filter by
mark, and sort by name or most recent. The bishopric and the approvers also see each person's
address and who decided and when, with the buttons: **Approve** / **Decline** for someone
waiting, **Revoke** to stop an approved volunteer signing up again (their current sign-ups
stay; remove them if needed) and **Undo decline** / **Undo revoke** (their next sign-up waits
for approval). The **Pre-approve list** (paste box, **Add**, the total, **Clear the list**) is
at the bottom of this tab for them.

**Change a post.** Open it, then **•••** > **Edit**. It opens with its dates in **Pick days**
(or **Once**, for one date). A date or start time with people signed up can't be moved or
removed until you remove them (or cancel that date, below); everyone signed up keeps their
place when you change other dates or roles. **•••** > **Copy this post** starts a new draft
from it.

**Close or cancel?** (in the post's **•••**, except Cancel this date)
- **Close**: no one new can sign up, but it is still happening. People already signed up keep
  their place and still get their reminder. (**Save as draft** on a published post does the
  same and also hides it from the public page.)
- **Cancel this opportunity**: it isn't happening. Everyone signed up gets an email (with your
  short note, if you add one), anyone still waiting simply drops off, no reminders go out, and
  it can't be reopened.
- **Cancel this date**: one date of a longer post isn't happening. The people signed up for that
  date get the email; the other dates carry on.

**Sign out everywhere.** If your link may have been forwarded, tap **•••** at the top of
Opportunities, then **Sign out everywhere**.
The old link stops working and a new one is emailed to you. Open the newest link on every device
you use (phone, computer). If a page is still open with an old link, it shows *This leader link
has expired…* and an **Email me a leader link** box at the top as soon as you come back to it or
open a form. Nothing you type there can be saved: copy it, then open the newest link.

**The buttons on a person's line** are, in order: **Approve** and **Decline** (someone waiting
for approval, if you may decide for them), **Remove**, and on the last line of each date
**Cancel this date** (posts with more than one date). On a phone they wrap onto the line under
the name. A post you see only because you approve for its kind shows names and marks, and
Approve / Decline where you may decide, but no Remove, Cancel or •••.

**The hub's QR codes.** On the ward's hub, each link's **Show QR Code** pop-up has **Print**: a
one-page poster with the link's title, a big QR code, the short link and the ward's name, for a
bulletin board or a table at church.

**Gemini.** Do not ask Gemini to read, summarize or draft from the Service Sheet, the request
sheets or request notices.

**Requests.** Requests for help never come to the leader page or your inbox. You get a short
email saying a request came in; open your **Requests** folder in Google Drive, where each
request is a row. Fill in **Status** (New, In progress, Done, Declined), **Assigned to** and a
short **Leader note**. Contact the person directly. Never copy request details into an email.
Requests are removed 30 days after they are Done or Declined.
- **The bishopric** (the bishop, both counselors, the Executive Secretary and the Ward Clerk)
  can open every request folder.
- **Christmas and Other requests** go only to the bishopric and anyone they name as that kind's
  coordinator, such as the Christmas gift coordinators. The email for a new Other request goes
  to the bishop.
- **Other kinds** (such as Moving) also go to the leaders of the organizations that own them
  and to their coordinators. The email for a new Moving request goes to those leaders and
  coordinators, not to the whole bishopric.

**Turning a request into an opportunity** (for example, the Relief Society president with a
*Lend a hand* request). The request itself never leaves its sheet; only a blank draft is made.
1. Read the request in its sheet (your **Requests** folder in Drive).
2. Tick **Make opportunity** on that row. Tick **Copy contact to private details** too only if
   the volunteers will need the requester's name and phone or email on the day (they go into
   the private details, which only leaders who can edit the post and approved, signed-up
   volunteers see; the note is never copied).
3. Within the hour **Posted as** says *Draft made …*, the box unticks itself, and a
   **Draft** marked *From a request* appears under **My opportunities** on your leader page.
   (If it says *Not made: set Opportunity category …*, ask the Executive Secretary to fill in
   that request kind's **Opportunity category**, then tick the box again.)
4. On the leader page, **Edit** the draft: check the date (a Moving date is copied; otherwise
   it is a week out), write the public note, set the roles and how many people, then
   **Publish**. Never put the family's name, address or situation in the public note.
5. In the request sheet, set **Status** to **In progress** or **Done** as usual.

A **Moving** request turned into an opportunity becomes an **Elders Quorum** draft (in the
*Moving* category, which the Elders Quorum owns). A Relief Society president who wants it should
ask the Elders Quorum president, or the bishopric can set Moving's **Opportunity category** to a
Relief Society category.

**Matching Christmas offers (for the Christmas gift coordinators).** Families offer gifts on the
Christmas giving form, from a link the ward shares or, if the ward turned it on, from the
*Offer to give at Christmas* card on the Service Opportunities page. Only you and the bishopric
see the offers. Each offer is a row in your **Requests – Christmas giving offers** folder, and
each family's request is a row in **Requests – Christmas help**. An offer can be as general as
"I want to give to another family this Christmas"; contact them when you have a family to
match.
1. Open both folders' sheets.
2. Pick a request and an offer that fit.
3. Contact the giver yourself. Share only what they need to give well, and no more than the
   family agreed to.
4. Record the match in both sheets: **Assigned to** and **Leader note** (e.g. "matched with
   offer o…" on the request, "request r…" on the offer, using the **Request id** column). Mark both **Done** when the gifts are
   delivered.
5. Closed rows are deleted 30 days after they are Done or Declined.
Never copy request or offer details into an email or into Gemini.

**The Service Sheet.** The bishopric edits it; other ward council members can view it. To change
Settings, Leaders or Categories, ask the Executive Secretary.
