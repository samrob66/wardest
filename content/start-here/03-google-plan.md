## What this gives the ward

Members will book their own interviews on Google Calendar booking pages (Google calls them appointment schedules). A free Google account can make only a basic booking page that checks one calendar. The interview setup in steps 4 to 6 needs two things a free account can't do: several booking pages, and a page that checks more than one calendar before it offers a time. One paid plan on the role account covers the whole bishopric.

## Steps

- **1.** Ask the bishop how the ward will pay for the plan before you buy it.
- **2.** On a computer, sign in to [one.google.com](https://one.google.com) as the role account.
- **3.** Compare the plans and pick one that includes premium appointment scheduling (booking pages). Google renames its plans from time to time; the plan's feature list should mention appointment schedules or booking pages. Wards using this setup have found the Google AI Plus plan enough.
- **4.** Subscribe on the role account, not on your own account.
- **5.** Check that it worked: in Google Calendar (as the role account), click Create, then Appointment schedule, and look for "Calendars checked for availability". On a paid plan you can add a second calendar there. You don't need to save anything yet.

## Done when

- The role account is on a paid plan that allows several booking pages.
- A booking page on the role account can check more than one calendar for availability.

## Notes

**Use a computer.** The booking-page settings you need, especially adding a second calendar to check, are only in Google Calendar on a desktop web browser. The phone app can't do it. This is the most common reason people get stuck.

**Write it down.** Record the plan, when it renews and who pays for it in the hand-off note (step 14), so it doesn't lapse when you are released.

If you ever downgrade the account, the extra booking pages stop working, and members will see fewer open times than you expect.
