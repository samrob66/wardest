## What this gives the ward

One Google account that belongs to the calling, not to you. The calendars, booking pages, short links, Zoom and trackers you set up in the next steps all live on it, so when you are released the next Executive Secretary gets everything by receiving one password.

You and the other leaders still sign in to WardForward with your own Google accounts. The role account is for the shared tools.

## Steps

- **1.** On a computer, go to [accounts.google.com/signup](https://accounts.google.com/signup) and create a new, free Google account.
- **2.** Name it for the calling, not for yourself. For example, first name "Ward" and last name "Bishopric", with an address like yourwardnamebishopric@gmail.com. Pick something the next bishopric will recognize.
- **3.** Choose a long password. Agree with the bishop where it will be kept (for example, written down and kept by the bishop, or in a password manager he can also open).
- **4.** Turn on 2-Step Verification: go to [myaccount.google.com](https://myaccount.google.com), open Security, then 2-Step Verification, and follow the prompts. Use your own phone for the codes for now.
- **5.** Still under Security, set the recovery phone and recovery email. Use your phone and the bishop's email, so that the account can be recovered even if you are unavailable.
- **6.** Open Google Calendar on this account once and check its time zone (Settings, then Time zone). It must be your ward's time zone.
- **7.** Right away, ask for zippa.run access from this account (see the note below).

## Done when

- A shared Google account exists for the calling, not for a person.
- 2-Step Verification is on.
- The recovery phone and recovery email are set, and the bishop knows how the next Executive Secretary takes them over.
- The bishop knows where the password is kept.
- The zippa.run access request has been sent from this account.

## Notes

**Send the zippa.run access request now.** zippa.run is the short-link service your ward will use for its QR codes (step 7). Each request is approved by hand, so it can take a few days. Go to [app.zippa.run](https://app.zippa.run), choose Sign in with Google, pick the role account, and fill in the form with your name, an email you read and a phone number. Step 7 picks up from there once you are approved.

When you hand the calling on, you give the next Executive Secretary the password, and they replace the 2-Step Verification phone and recovery details with their own (step 14 covers the hand-off).

Never use the role account for anything personal, and never keep member records in it.
