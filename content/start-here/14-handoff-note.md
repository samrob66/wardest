## What this gives the ward

Callings change every few years. Because everything lives on the role account, a new Executive Secretary can take it all over in an afternoon, as long as someone wrote down what exists and where. This note is that list.

## Steps

- **1.** Signed in as the role account, open [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com), click New, then Google Docs, and name it "Hand-off note: ward tools".
- **2.** List every account and tool, using the outline below. For each one write what it is, where it lives (its address), and who can change it.
- **3.** Write down where the role account's password is kept, never the password itself.
- **4.** Add the "When a leader is released" and "When the Executive Secretary is released" checklists below.
- **5.** Share the note with the bishop. Update it whenever you change something.

## What to list

- The role account: its address, who keeps the password, and who holds the 2-Step Verification phone and recovery details.
- The Google plan: which plan, when it renews and who pays.
- The calendars: the account's own (staging) calendar and each leader's interview calendar, and who each is shared with.
- The booking pages: each page's title and link.
- The interview router: the "Interview Router" project at script.google.com and its every-minute trigger.
- zippa.run: the ward's prefix and the list of short links and where each points.
- WardForward: the ward's administrators, the public portal's address and share link, and the service section links.
- The foyer flyer: where the current PDF is and where it's posted.
- Zoom: that it's on the role account, and the privacy settings.
- The assignment rotation trackers (steps 12 and 13): each one's Sheet, web app link and where its PIN is kept.

## When a leader is released

- Re-share that leader's interview calendar with the new leader's email and remove the old share. Booking pages, the router and the short links don't change, because they use the calling, not the name.
- Change any booking-page description that names the leader.
- In WardForward, invite the new leader with the same calling and spaces. Tell the operator if the released leader should lose access now.
- Update the leader lists in the assignment rotation trackers.

## When the Executive Secretary is released

- Give the new Executive Secretary the password (or where it's kept).
- They sign in, set up their own 2-Step Verification, and replace the recovery phone and email with theirs.
- The bishop makes the new Executive Secretary a Superadmin in WardForward.
- Walk through this note together.

## Done when

- A note in the role account's Drive lists every account and tool and where each lives.
- It says what to change when a leader is released and when the Executive Secretary is released.
- The bishop can open it.

## Notes

Keep the note to tools and accounts. Never put member information, interview details or the password itself in it.
