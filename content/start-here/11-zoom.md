## What this gives the ward

When a member needs to meet online, the leader and the member each click one link and are in the meeting together, with nobody stuck waiting to be let in. Recording, transcripts and AI features are off for every meeting.

## Why not Google Meet

A Google Meet link's host is always the event's organizer, which is the role account. The role account never joins as a live person, so nobody can let the member in, and the leader can't either. Zoom can make meetings that anyone with the link can join, with no host needed.

## One-time setup

- **1.** Go to [zoom.us](https://zoom.us), choose Sign in with Google, and pick the role account. There's no separate Zoom password to hand on. The free plan is enough (meetings up to 40 minutes).
- **2.** In Zoom's Settings, turn Waiting Room off and "Join before host" on. Together these let anyone with the link join.
- **3.** Still in Settings, turn these off: Recording ("Record to computer files" and "Automatic recording"), meeting transcripts, "Allow participants to transcribe meetings with My Notes", the AI Companion features, "Enable Screenshot feature in meeting chat", and under Zoom Apps "Share realtime meeting content with apps".
- **4.** In the Chrome browser, install the Zoom Chrome Extension from the Chrome Web Store and sign in to it as the role account. It adds a "Make it a Zoom Meeting" button inside Google Calendar. (Don't use the "Zoom for Google Workspace" add-on; it fails to sign in on free accounts.)
- **5.** In Google Calendar, open each booking page and make sure "Add Google Meet video conferencing" is unticked.

## Each time someone wants to meet online

- **1.** In Chrome, open the member's booking in Google Calendar and click Edit (the pencil).
- **2.** Click "Make it a Zoom Meeting", then Save. The member gets the updated invitation with the link.
- **3.** At the time, the leader and the member both click the link.

## Done when

- Zoom is set up on the role account.
- Meetings are open-join (Waiting Room off, "Join before host" on).
- Recording, transcripts and AI features are off.

## Notes

**Never keep interview details.** Don't record, transcribe or write up what is said in an interview, in Zoom, in WardForward or anywhere else. Follow the General Handbook (38.8.32) on keeping such matters confidential. WardForward never stores interview content.

**Be honest about the limit.** No video service can stop someone recording their own screen or pointing a phone at it. These settings remove the easy, built-in ways; the rest is the same trust an in-person interview relies on.

Screen sharing, chat and the whiteboard are not a privacy risk; leave them on if leaders find them useful. Zoom changes its settings from time to time, so test a meeting once before you rely on it.
