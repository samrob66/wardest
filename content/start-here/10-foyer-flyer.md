## What this gives the ward

A printed page or poster in the foyer, on the bulletin board or in the bishop's waiting area, with a QR code for each link and one big QR code for the whole portal. Members scan it with their phone camera. Because every code uses a short link, the flyer never needs reprinting when a link changes.

## Steps

- **1.** Do steps 7 to 9 first, so every link has its short link and the portal has its share link. The big QR code uses the share link.
- **2.** On the ward home page, under Portals, open "Public", and click "print" at the top (next to "ward home"). It opens a Letter-size flyer in a new tab.
- **3.** For a bigger size, add one of these to the end of that page's address and press Enter: ?size=tabloid (11 by 17 inches), ?size=a4, ?size=poster-18x24 or ?size=poster-24x36.
- **4.** Print it from the browser (Ctrl+P, or Cmd+P on a Mac). Choose "Save as PDF" if a print shop will print the poster. If colors are missing, turn on "Background graphics" in the print settings.
- **5.** Scan every QR code on the printout with a phone before you post it.
- **6.** Post it where members wait, with the bishop's approval of where it goes.

## Done when

- A flyer or poster is printed from the portal's print view.
- Its QR codes were tested with a phone.
- It is posted in the building.

## Notes

**Changing a link later** happens on zippa.run only. Reprint only when you add or remove links.

**Hand-outs.** The Letter-size flyer also works as a hand-out for new members and new move-ins.

Keep a PDF of the current flyer in the role account's Drive and note it in the hand-off note (step 14).
