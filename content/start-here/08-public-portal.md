## What this gives the ward

One public page with every link members need (bulletin, forms, chat, interview booking pages), each with a QR code, plus one big QR code for the whole page. Members bookmark one address; you can change any link without reprinting anything.

## Steps

- **1.** On this page, set the status to In progress and click Save status. A Deliverables section appears below it.
- **2.** Under "Add a link", fill in the Title members will see (for example "Ward Bulletin"), the real address (URL), and the short link you made in step 7 (for example zippa.run/abcBulletin). Click "Add link + generate QR".
- **3.** In the new link's card, choose "Public" in the list and click Publish. (The list starts on Bishopric; make sure it says Public.)
- **4.** Repeat for each link, including each interview booking page.
- **5.** Go back to your ward's home page and open the Public portal (the "/p/your-prefix" link) to see it as members will.
- **6.** On the ward home page under Portals, open "Public". Under "Portal sections", add a section such as "Interviews" with Add section, then under "Which section each link is in" move the booking pages into it with Move. Links you don't move show under "Links".
- **7.** Make a zippa.run short link (for example abcLinks) that points at the portal address shown under "Share link (optional)". Paste it there and click Save share link.

## Done when

- Each link is a deliverable published to Public with its short link pasted.
- The portal's share link is set.

## Notes

**Changing a link later.** Change where the short link points on zippa.run. The portal, its QR codes and every printed flyer keep working. If you forgot to add a short link, use "Save short link" on the link's card.

**The portal is public.** Anyone with the address can see it (search engines are asked not to list it). Never put members' personal information or private documents on it. WardForward shows its independence notice at the bottom of every page for you.

**You don't need to build a website.** Some wards built their own links site before WardForward; WardForward's portal replaces that. The WardForward demo site at [github.com/wardforward](https://github.com/wardforward) is only an example a ward may copy if it wants one.
