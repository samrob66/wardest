## What this gives the ward

A web page for each kind of interview where a member picks an open time on their own: settings apart, temple recommends, time with the bishop, tithing declarations. Members stop waiting on phone calls and texts, and each leader's calendar fills itself.

## The one rule

Every booking page's title must contain its leader's calling tag, exactly, in square brackets: [Bishop], [1st Counselor] or [2nd Counselor]. The interview router (step 6) reads that tag to know whose calendar the booking belongs on. Use the same tag on every page for that leader.

## Steps (repeat for each page)

- **1.** On a computer, in Google Calendar as the role account, click Create, then Appointment schedule.
- **2.** Title: the kind of interview, then the tag. For example "Settings Apart [1st Counselor]". Keep it short; long titles get cut off.
- **3.** Appointment duration: 10 minutes for settings apart and tithing declarations, 20 minutes for temple recommends; the bishop's own pages are 15, 20 and 30 minutes.
- **4.** General availability: when that leader holds interviews, for example Sundays 12:00 to 1:00 PM.
- **5.** Scheduling window: for example up to 60 days ahead. Minimum notice: for example 2 hours before. Buffer: none.
- **6.** Under "Calendars checked for availability", add that leader's calendar (for example "1st Counselor Interviews"). The account's own calendar is already checked and can't be unchecked; leave it.
- **7.** Booking form: ask for the member's name. Email is optional.
- **8.** Description: "Please select the earliest time slot that aligns with your availability." followed by where the offices are in your building. Asking for the earliest slot keeps appointments packed together.
- **9.** Untick "Add Google Meet video conferencing" (Meet doesn't work with this setup; step 11 explains Zoom instead).
- **10.** Save, open the booking page, and copy its calendar.app.google link. Keep a list of every page's link; you need them in steps 7 and 8.

## A typical set of pages

- Settings Apart [1st Counselor] and Settings Apart [2nd Counselor], 10 minutes.
- Temple Recommends [1st Counselor] and Temple Recommends [2nd Counselor], 20 minutes.
- Interview 15 min [Bishop], Interview 20 min [Bishop] (also for first-time temple recommends) and Interview 30 min [Bishop].
- Tithing Declaration [Bishop], 10 minutes. Tithing declarations are with the bishop only.

## Done when

- Every kind of appointment has a booking page whose title contains its leader's calling tag in square brackets.
- Each page checks that leader's calendar as well as the account's own.
- You have a list of every page's calendar.app.google link.

## Notes

**Times look an hour off?** A booking page shows times in the viewer's time zone (top right of the page). Check that the role account's time zone is your ward's.

**When a leader is released,** don't make new pages: the tag is the calling, so the pages, their links and every QR code stay the same. Change the page's description if it names the leader.

Booking pages collect members' names. Treat that list with the same care as any ward record.
