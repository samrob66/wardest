## What this gives the ward

Every interview lands on the right leader's own calendar, so each leader's open times are independent: a member booking the 1st Counselor doesn't take that time away from the 2nd Counselor or the bishop. Each leader sees their own interviews on their phone.

## How it works

Google always saves a booking to the role account's own calendar first. You can't change that. So the account's own calendar becomes a temporary "staging" area that stays empty, each leader gets a calendar of their own, and the interview router (step 6) moves each new booking onto the right leader's calendar within a minute.

## Steps

- **1.** On a computer, sign in to Google Calendar as the role account.
- **2.** In the left column, find "Other calendars", click the + next to it, and choose "Create new calendar".
- **3.** Create one calendar per leader, named for the calling, not the person: "Bishop Interviews", "1st Counselor Interviews" and "2nd Counselor Interviews". Click Create calendar for each.
- **4.** Share each calendar with its leader: hover over the calendar in the left column, click the three dots, then "Settings and sharing". Under "Share with specific people or groups", add the leader's own email and choose "See all event details".
- **5.** Give each calendar its own color (the three dots again), so bookings are easy to tell apart.
- **6.** Leave the account's own calendar (it shows the account's name) alone. Don't add anything to it; it is the staging calendar.

## Done when

- There is one interview calendar per leader, the bishop included.
- Each calendar is shared with its leader.
- The account's own calendar is left empty.

## Notes

**Why the bishop gets his own calendar too.** If the bishop used the account's own calendar, his bookings would block the counselors. With his own calendar, his times can overlap theirs.

**Why calling names.** When a counselor is released, nothing needs renaming: you only change who the calendar is shared with (step 14 lists this).

**Keep the staging calendar visible on the role account.** If you untick the account's own calendar in the left column, the "Booking pages" list disappears from the sidebar too. Nothing is deleted; tick it again and they come back. Leaders looking at their own calendars can hide it safely.
