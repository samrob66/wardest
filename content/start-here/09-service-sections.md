## What this gives the ward

Two sections at the top of the public portal. "Service Opportunities" lists the ways members can help right now (meals, moves, cleaning the building and more) and lets them sign up. "Service Requests" lets a member ask for help, privately, and the right leaders are told. Each section has its own short link and QR code, so you can also share it on its own.

## Steps

- **1.** On your ward's home page, find "Portal service sections". Under each field it shows a "Direct address" (ending in /serve/your-prefix and /help/your-prefix).
- **2.** On zippa.run, make two short links pointing at those two direct addresses, for example zippa.run/abcServe and zippa.run/abcHelp (with your own prefix in place of "abc").
- **3.** Back in "Portal service sections", paste them into "Service Opportunities link" and "Service Requests link". Tick both "Show ... on the portal" boxes and click Save.
- **4.** Open "Service opportunities & requests" from the ward home page. Under "Categories you manage", open each category. The line under its name says who owns it (for example "owned by Relief Society").
- **5.** For each category, check that the owning space has its leaders as Owners (step 2). The owners of that space manage the category and get its messages.
- **6.** Optional: under "Coordinators" (for opportunities) or "People who help with these requests" (for requests), pick a ward member and click Add, or invite someone by email. Coordinators run that category's opportunities for its owners.
- **7.** Optional: to announce new opportunities in a Discord channel, open the category's "Discord channel" section and paste the channel's webhook link (in Discord: Server Settings, Integrations, Webhooks), then Save.

## The categories every ward starts with

- Opportunities: Provide a Meal (Relief Society), Lend a Helping Hand (Ward Council), Help with an Activity (Activities Committee), Clean the Building (Bishopric), Clean the Temple (Relief Society and Elders Quorum), Substitute in Primary (Primary), Substitute in Sunday School (Sunday School), Help Someone Move (in/out) (Elders Quorum).
- Requests: Get Help Moving (Elders Quorum), Request a Christmas or Presents (Bishopric, confidential), Other Requests (Bishopric, confidential).

## Done when

- Both service sections are on.
- Each section has its zippa.run short link pasted in "Portal service sections".
- Each default category's owning space has its leaders as Owners.
- Optional: coordinators named, and a Discord channel webhook pasted where an organization wants one.

## Notes

**Confidential requests.** Christmas and Other requests are seen only by the bishopric space's owners, the people named on the category and superadmins. The emails leaders get say a request came in, never what it says.

**Keep the webhook private.** A Discord webhook link lets anyone post to that channel. WardForward never shows it in full once saved.

Requests are deleted 30 days after they are closed.
