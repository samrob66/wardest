## What this gives the ward

Every leader signs in to WardForward with their own Google account and sees what belongs to their calling. Each organization gets its own space (Elders Quorum, Relief Society, Primary and so on), and the Bishopric and Ward Council spaces hold what those councils share. Doing this early lets the bishopric help with the later steps.

## Before you start

The bishop must approve the ward using WardForward (General Handbook 38.8.21.2). Ask him first.

## Steps

- **1.** Sign in at [app.wardforward.com](https://app.wardforward.com) with your own Google account (not the role account).
- **2.** Click Request a workspace. Fill in Ward name, Unit number and Your calling, tick "Our bishop has approved using WardForward for our ward", and click Submit request. A WardForward operator approves each new ward. If your ward is already on WardForward, your request goes to that ward's admins instead.
- **3.** Once approved, open your ward. You are its first Superadmin (WardForward's word for a ward administrator).
- **4.** Under "Invite a leader (callings chart)", invite the bishop: his Email, Calling "Bishop", Ward role "Superadmin", Space "Bishopric", Space role "Owner". Click Create / update invite. He gets an email with a link to accept; the same link shows under "Pending invites".
- **5.** Invite the counselors and the Ward Clerk to the Bishopric space the same way, with Ward role "Member".
- **6.** Invite each organization's president and secretary to their own organization's space with Space role "Owner". The owners of a space manage it.
- **7.** Add the ward council members to the Ward Council space too. To put someone in a second space, submit the invite form again with the same email and the other space; it adds another space.
- **8.** Under "Members & roles", check that both the bishop and you show "superadmin". If not, click Make superadmin.

## Done when

- The bishop approved the ward using WardForward (General Handbook 38.8.21.2).
- The bishop and the Executive Secretary are both Superadmins, so the ward has at least two administrators.
- The bishopric and the ward council are invited with their callings.
- Each organization's president and secretary are Owners of their organization's space.
- The Ward Clerk is in the Bishopric space.

## Notes

**Keep at least two administrators.** WardForward will not let the last Superadmin step down, but the ward should always have two: the bishop and the Executive Secretary.

**Activities and missionary work.** An Activities committee chair or a Ward Mission Leader who is not in the Ward Council space sees only their own organization's service posts. Add them to the Ward Council space if they should see the others.

**Spaces you don't use.** Every ward gets the same list of spaces. Ignore the ones your ward doesn't use; there is no screen yet to hide or rename them.

**Leaders who change callings.** WardForward has no screen yet to remove someone from a space. Invite the new leader into the same space, and tell the operator if a released leader should lose access now.
