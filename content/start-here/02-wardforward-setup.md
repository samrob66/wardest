## Done when

- The bishop approved the ward using WardForward (General Handbook 38.8.21.2).
- The bishop and the Executive Secretary are ward admins, so the ward has at least two administrators.
- The bishopric and ward council are invited with their callings.
- Each organization's president and secretary own their organization's space.
- The Ward Clerk is in the Bishopric space.
- An Activities chair or Ward Mission Leader outside the Ward Council space sees only their own organization's posts until they are added.
